Smart Healthcare Diagnostics

Date May 2025 - Jul 2025
Category Edge-Cloud ML
Status Completed
Flutter AWS TensorFlow Keras Agile

Overview

Engineered a PoC Flutter-based Edge-Cloud Heart Monitoring System on AWS applying Agile methodologies. Integrating tiered TensorFlow AI models for remote diagnostics—one running on the edge to be used offline without internet, and another heavier model on the Cloud.

Technical Implementation & Results

Achieved 93.21% Cloud and 85.25% Edge prediction accuracy for remote diagnostics by improving a pre-trained Keras model and optimizing its hyperparameters.
