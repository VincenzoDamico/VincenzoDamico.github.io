Agent-Based Q-Learning Environment (Thesis Project)

Date Nov 2024 - Mar 2025
Category Reinforcement Learning
Status Completed
Repast Simphony Java Q-Learning Multi-Agent Systems

Overview

Engineered a multi-agent Reinforcement Learning environment using Java and Repast Simphony. The core objective was training and evaluating AI models across dynamic 3x3 and 4x4 grid configurations.

Results & Innovation

While testing, the AI model experienced a 40% win-rate stagnation due to polarization. I entirely resolved this bottleneck by conceptualizing and designing a custom "double-step" Q-table update algorithm. This algorithm accurately integrates opponent states into the reward loop, dramatically improving decision accuracy.
